What Trackrr is

Trackrr is a personal tool for keeping track of your bills, spending and budgets. It isn't a bank, a payment service, or a financial, tax or accounting adviser, and it can't move your money.

Check anything important

Trackrr reads documents written by other people, so it can misread a statement, miss a transaction or send a reminder late. Your bank's and card issuer's records are the official ones. Paying your bills on time remains your responsibility.

Your accounts and your Mac
