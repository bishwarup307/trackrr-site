Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Your financial information stays on your Mac. Trackrr has no servers to receive it, so we never see it.

Trackrr is a Mac app for tracking bills, spending and budgets, made by an independent developer (“we” on this page). This policy explains what the app does with your information, including data from your Google account. Trackrr is in early development, so some features mentioned here are still being built.

What Trackrr reads from Google

When you connect a Gmail account, Trackrr asks Google for one permission: read-only access to your email (gmail.readonly). It then reads:

  • Your Gmail address, to show which account is connected.
  • Email details, such as the sender and subject, to find your bank and card statements, transaction alerts and purchase receipts.
  • The content and attachments of those emails, to read your transactions, amounts and due dates, and to recognise which bank accounts and cards you have.

Google offers no narrower permission for reading messages and attachments, so this one covers the whole mailbox. Trackrr doesn't keep or use emails that aren't financial.

The permission can't send, change or delete your email. You sign in on Google's own page, and Trackrr never sees your Google password.

Statements you add yourself

You can also give Trackrr a statement by dragging a PDF into it or choosing one. Trackrr can open only the files you give it, and only to read them. It keeps the statements among them with your other records, in the encrypted files described below.

How Trackrr uses your information

Only to provide the features you see in the app, such as your spending record, budgets, bill reminders and matching receipts to charges.

  • It's never sold or shared.
  • It's never used for advertising.
  • It's never used to train AI models.
  • It's never read by people, because we never receive it.

Where your information is kept

On your Mac, in Trackrr's private folder:

  • Your financial records, and the emails and PDFs they came from, are kept in encrypted files.
  • Your Google sign-in permissions and the passwords you enter for locked statements are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Trackrr doesn't put your information in iCloud. If you back up your Mac with Time Machine, your backup includes Trackrr's encrypted files.

What leaves your Mac

Your financial information doesn't. Trackrr has no account system, no servers, no analytics and no tracking.

Trackrr does connect to Google, directly from your Mac, to sign you in and collect your email. Google handles those requests under its own privacy policy. Trackrr sends your information to no other service, including cloud AI services. Any AI feature runs entirely on your Mac.

A future version may check for updates or a valid licence, or offer crash reports that you choose to send. None of these would carry your financial information, and we'll update this page before adding them.

Deleting your information

Your information stays on your Mac until you delete it. Because we never receive it, we can't delete it or recover it for you.

  • Disconnect an account in Trackrr. This asks Google to end Trackrr's access and removes the sign-in permission from your Mac.
  • Remove access from Google's side. You can do this at any time in your Google Account.
  • Delete everything. This option in Trackrr removes its data and Keychain items and disconnects Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Trackrr's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site uses no cookies or analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions are welcome on our GitHub issues page. Anything posted there is public, so please don't include financial or personal details.
